# Cuora housing and outdoor sunning

**Scope:** Enclosure layout for the golden coin box turtle
(*Cuora trifasciata*, three-striped box turtle) and the conditions under which
occasional outdoor placement is safe. Excludes diet, breeding and disease.

## Conclusion

**Keep the animal in one fixed enclosure year-round.** Never shuttle it between
two enclosures on a daily day/night cycle — repeated relocation is a chronic
stressor and offers no benefit.

Main enclosure: **60% water, 40% land**, with water depth **1–2× carapace
height** so the animal can breathe with its head up while standing on the bottom.

**Occasional outdoor sunning is acceptable only if all of these hold:**

1. **More than half the container is in full shade** at all times
2. Water depth **1.5–2× carapace height** in a large total volume — thick plastic
   bin, ceramic vat or similar, never a shallow tray
3. The animal is never left unattended where the sun can move onto full coverage

The failure mode is fatal and fast: a shallow container in direct sun becomes a
cooking vessel within an hour.

## Verify

```
Before placing the animal, fill the outdoor container and leave it in the
  intended spot for one hour. Measure the water temperature.
  If it has risen more than a few degrees, the shade or the volume is inadequate.
Check shade coverage again two hours later — the sun will have moved.
```

## Facts

- *C. trifasciata* is **semi-aquatic and water-leaning**: it needs standing water
  it can rest in, not merely a dish.
- *C. flavomarginata* by contrast is **highly terrestrial** — damp forest floor,
  burrows into leaf litter. The two species have different requirements and must
  not be housed together.
- Large water volume buffers temperature swings; small volumes do not.

## Rejected

| Option | Reason |
|---|---|
| Two enclosures on a daily day/night rotation | Chronic relocation stress with no compensating benefit |
| A shallow tray outdoors | Heats fast in sun and gives the animal nowhere to escape to |
| Less than half the container shaded | The sun moves; partial shade becomes no shade |
