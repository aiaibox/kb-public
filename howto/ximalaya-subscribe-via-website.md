# Ximalaya: subscribe via website, not iOS

**Scope:** Where to buy a Ximalaya (喜马拉雅) membership and why the channel
matters. Prices are as observed and will drift.

## Conclusion

**Subscribe through the official website, not iOS in-app purchase** — iOS prices
carry Apple's channel commission.

iOS in-app prices, verbatim:

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| 连续包月 (auto-renew monthly) | ¥20 |
| 连续包季 (auto-renew quarterly) | ¥58 |
| 连续包年 (auto-renew yearly) | **¥178** |
| One month, non-auto-renewing | ¥25 |
| One year, non-auto-renewing | ¥238–268 |

**The website publishes no fixed price list.** Membership cost appears only
through current promotions and bundle offers on the page, so the actual web price
must be read at purchase time and compared against the ¥178/year iOS auto-renew
figure. That comparison is the whole decision — there is no standing web price to
quote.

## Verify

```
On iPhone, Ximalaya app → VIP/member purchase page: confirm 连续包月 ¥20,
  连续包季 ¥58, 连续包年 ¥178; one-off month ¥25; one-off year ¥238-268.
Open https://www.ximalaya.com in a browser, sign in, go to the membership/VIP
  page: confirm no static price table exists, read the promo price shown, and
  compare with ¥178/year.
```

## Facts

- Auto-renewing iOS: ¥20/month, ¥58/quarter, ¥178/year (RMB).
- Non-auto-renewing iOS: ¥25 for one month; ¥238–268 for one year. Note the
  non-auto-renewing yearly price is **higher** than the auto-renewing one — the
  discount is for accepting auto-renewal, not for buying longer.
- No unified standard membership price is published on the website; pricing
  surfaces only via live promotions or bundles.

## Rejected

| Option | Reason |
|---|---|
| iOS in-app purchase | Prices include Apple's channel commission. ¥178/year in-app versus potentially cheaper web promotions |
| Looking up a fixed web price list in advance | There isn't one. The attempt fails by design, not by search error — the site shows only current promotions |

## Open

- Whether the web subscription is actually cheaper than ¥178/year, and by how
  much, is unresolved — it depends on the promotion live at the time.
- No direct purchase URL; navigate from https://www.ximalaya.com to the paid
  option.
