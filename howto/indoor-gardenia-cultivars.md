# Indoor gardenia cultivars

**Scope:** A shortlist of gardenia cultivars for indoor containers, organised by
what you are optimising for, plus the indoor care conditions gardenias actually
require. Excludes outdoor landscape use and propagation.

## Conclusion

**If you want fragrance, choose 'Veitchii' or 'Jubilation'. If space is the
constraint, choose 'Buttons' or 'Kleim's Hardy'.**

### Smallest size / lowest light tolerance

| Cultivar | Notes |
|---|---|
| **'Radicans'** | Trailing dwarf, typically 1–2 ft (some sources 6–12"), spreading low habit — the classic container gardenia |
| **'Kleim's Hardy'** | Compact, under 3 ft; star-shaped single white flowers with bright yellow centres; **resists stretching in lower light** |
| **'Buttons'** | Highly compact, rounded, minimal root volume |
| **'Radicans Variegata'** | Same compact size as 'Radicans', variegated foliage |
| **'Fragrant Pathways'** | Groundcover habit, 8–10", low and dense — good for shallow pots |

### Best flower size and plant shape

| Cultivar | Notes |
|---|---|
| **'Aimee'** (Aimee Yoshioka) | The gold standard for flower size — exceptionally large double rose-like white blooms |
| **'August Beauty'** | Very upright, symmetrical, architectural habit |
| **'Mystery'** | Large traditional double flowers, heavy dense petals — but root-space heavy for containers |
| **'Crown Jewel'** | Refined upright hybrid, tidy non-straggly form |
| **'First Love'** | Very large early-season white flowers, strong upright branching |

### Best fragrance and longest bloom

| Cultivar | Bloom window |
|---|---|
| **'Veitchii'** | The most reliable everblooming gardenia — near-continuous with proper light and cool nights |
| **'Jubilation'** | Compact, ~3 ft; reblooms late spring through first frost |
| **'ScentAmazing'** | Compact, ~3 ft; highly fragrant, long window, consistent reblooming |
| **'Fool Proof'** (patent name **'Leesix'**) | Reblooms late spring into autumn |
| **'Daisy'** | Late spring through early autumn |
| **'Pinwheel'** | Narrow-petalled, potent scent, late spring to early autumn |
| **'Florida'** | Medium, 3–4 ft; the traditional florist gardenia, classic high-intensity fragrance |

### Care — the part that decides whether any of them bloom

| | |
|---|---|
| **Light** | South or west window, at least a few hours of direct morning or filtered afternoon sun |
| **Cool nights** | **~65–70°F day, ~60°F night.** This temperature cycle triggers bud development and extra bloom flushes — even the most low-light-tolerant cultivar performs significantly better with it. A climate with naturally cool nights gives this for free indoors |
| **Humidity** | **50–70%** — pebble tray or nearby humidifier |
| **Soil pH** | **4.5–6.0.** Use a mix for acid-loving plants (azalea or camellia mix) |
| **Water** | **Filtered, distilled or rainwater.** Hard tap water minerals raise soil pH over time and cause nutrient lockout |

## Verify

```
Before purchase, confirm each cultivar name and its stated size and bloom
  window in a horticultural database (RHS Plant Finder, or Gardenia.net
  cultivar pages).
Check trade name against patent name: 'Fool Proof' should be listed with
  cultivar name 'Leesix'.
After purchase, confirm reblooming: the plant should set buds in multiple
  flushes from late spring onward. If not, increase window light before
  concluding the cultivar is wrong.
```

## Diagnosis

- **Yellowing leaves with green veins** → soil pH is too high (alkaline). Usually
  hard tap water accumulating over months.
- **Aphids and scale** → the plant is stressed; stressed indoor gardenias attract
  both. Treat with damp-cloth wipe-downs or mild insecticidal soap, and fix the
  underlying stressor.

## Rejected

| Option | Reason |
|---|---|
| Older large varieties ('Mystery', 'Belmont') for indoor containers | They demand massive root space; compact stability-bred cultivars suit pots |
| Large-leaf varieties in low-light positions | Prone to stretching; compact forms like 'Kleim's Hardy' resist it |
| Non-reblooming traditional varieties when fragrance duration matters | Much shorter bloom season than repeat-bloomers like 'Veitchii' and 'Jubilation' |

## Open

- 'Mystery' appears in the flower-form group but is flagged as root-space heavy —
  its container suitability is genuinely unresolved, not merely untested.
