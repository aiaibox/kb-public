# Checked luggage: 100–120 gauge stretch film + padding

**Source:** ChatGPT — https://chatgpt.com/c/6a851ab5-6054-83e8-b66f-5546fef5a8ee
**Exported:** 2026-09-05T20:59:09.336Z
**Turns:** 7

**Scope:** DIY protection for a checked or gate-checked suitcase (stretch film, internal padding, what not to pack); excludes brand-specific case advice and hard-case repair.

## Conclusion

Protect a checked carry-on as follows: (1) fill the case with soft items (clothes, sweaters, towels, foam, bubble wrap) packed tight against the corners and lid — no large internal voids, but do not overstuff to the point the shell or zipper is stressed; (2) fully retract the handle, remove or secure loose straps/tags, lock the TSA lock; (3) wrap 3–5 complete layers of moving stretch film in multiple directions so it cannot simply peel off — use 100–120 gauge (≈1.0–1.2 mil) film, 18–20 in wide; the recommended roll is 20 in × 1,000 ft × 100–120 gauge; reinforce corners and the zipper area and do not build a bulky wrap around the wheels; (4) keep the luggage tag visible and also put name/phone number inside the case. The film prevents scratches, scuffs, dirt, water and accidental zipper opening but does NOT protect against hard impact — the internal padding carries the impact load. Optionally add an elastic luggage cover under the film. Never check valuables: laptops, tablets, cameras, jewelry, cash, important documents, medicine, fragile electronics; if electronics must go in, place them centered, wrapped in clothes, not touching the shell. The film is not a security seal — TSA/security can cut it open for inspection.

## Verify

```
Roll label check: width 18–20 in and gauge 100–120 (100 gauge = 1.0 mil, 120 gauge = 1.2 mil); household cling film at ~60–90 gauge is too thin and is rejected
Coverage test on a 24 × 16 × 10 in carry-on: 20 in film should fully enclose it in ~4–6 overlapping passes; a 5 in hand roll needs many more overlapping passes and is slower/less uniform
```

## Decided

Buy DIY moving stretch film (20 in × 1,000 ft × 100–120 gauge) instead of paying for airport luggage wrapping; 100 gauge is the sweet spot, 120 gauge for an expensive case (e.g. Rimowa, Tumi), extra wraps at corners and zipper.

## Facts

- Household cling film: ~60–90 gauge — too thin for checked luggage
- Film gauges: light stretch wrap 60–80; standard moving film 80–100; heavy-duty 100–120 (best DIY choice); airport luggage wrap ~80–120+
- 100 gauge ≈ 1.0 mil; 120 gauge ≈ 1.2 mil
- Airport luggage wrap roll: 20–24 in (50–61 cm) wide × ~150–300 ft (46–91 m)
- Moving stretch wrap: 5–20 in (13–51 cm) wide × ~300–1,500 ft (91–457 m); large rolls 18–20 in (46–51 cm) × 500–1,500 ft at 80–120 gauge; hand rolls 5 in (13 cm) × 1,000–1,500 ft at 70–100 gauge
- A typical 24 × 16 × 10 in carry-on needs ~4–6 passes with 20 in film for reasonable coverage
- Part vulnerability ranking: corners and wheels highest risk, then handle and zipper, then center body, then TSA lock lowest; zipper pull should be locked and handle/straps fully retracted
- Recommended wrap order: lock and secure case → pad inside → (optional elastic cover) → 3–5 layers of stretch film in multiple directions

## Rejected

| Option | Reason |
|---|---|
| Airport luggage wrapping service | Same protection class as DIY moving film but higher cost and no DIY convenience |
| Household plastic/cling wrap as the only protection | ~60–90 gauge, too thin to survive baggage handling |
| Light stretch wrap (60–80 gauge) | Not ideal for checked-luggage handling stress |
| 5 in hand roll of moving film | Requires many more overlapping passes on a suitcase — slow and less uniform coverage |
| Treating the film as a security seal | TSA/security can cut it open for inspection, so it must not be relied on as a tamper seal |
| Relying on film for impact protection | Film only covers scratches, dirt and water; hard impact must be absorbed by internal padding |

## Failures

- **Wrapping film tears during handling** — Suitcases with protruding wheels puncture the film in transit — cover wheels lightly and never build a bulky package around them
