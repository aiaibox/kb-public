# CMU Pre-College selectivity by track

**Scope:** Acceptance rates and application requirements for Carnegie Mellon's
Pre-College summer programs, by track. Excludes CMU undergraduate admissions,
other universities' programs, and current-cycle deadlines.

## Conclusion

CMU Pre-College is selective but **far easier than CMU undergraduate admission**.
Overall acceptance across all tracks is roughly **25–35%** — but that aggregate
hides a 10× spread between tracks:

| Track group | Rate | What it weighs |
|---|---|---|
| **Fully funded** — AI Scholars (AIS), Computer Science Scholars (CSS), Summer Academy for Math and Science (SAMS) | **~3–10%**; AIS and CSS routinely **under 5–6%** | Exceptional math background; targets students from groups underrepresented in STEM. Covers tuition, room and board |
| **Specialised studio / technical** — National High School Game Academy (NHSGA), Computational Biology, Architecture, Design, Drama | **~15–25%** | Portfolios, auditions and prerequisites weighed heavily alongside grades |
| **Summer Session and general open-enrollment** | **~30–40%** | Most accessible for applicants meeting prerequisites |

**The single most important caveat: attending does not help with CMU
undergraduate admission.** Admissions officers treat paid pre-college programs as
enrichment, not a back-door entrance. Anyone applying as an admissions strategy
is applying for the wrong reason.

To be competitive:

- **GPA** typically **3.5+ unweighted**, with rigorous AP/IB coursework —
  math and science emphasis for STEM tracks
- **Essays**, which CMU weights heavily: why this field, readiness for college
  rigour, unique perspective
- **1–2 recommendation letters** from teachers or counsellors, speaking to work
  ethic and maturity
- **Supplemental portfolio** for Fine Arts, Architecture, Drama and the Game Academy
- **Standardised tests optional** for almost all programs — submit only if strong

## Verify

```
curl -sI https://www.cmu.edu/pre-college/
  Confirm the official site is live, then read it for current-cycle
  deadlines and requirements.
Check each track's own admissions page (AIS, CSS, SAMS, NHSGA, Summer
  Session) for stated eligibility and required materials BEFORE relying
  on any rate below.
```

## Open

- **CMU does not publish official per-track acceptance rates.** Every percentage
  above is an informal estimate — treat as ballpark and re-verify per cycle.
- Deadlines and program dates are not covered here and change annually.
- Detailed portfolio and audition expectations for NHSGA and the arts tracks are
  not covered.
